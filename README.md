# Hollow Vale

A vanilla-friendly dark biome of skull mobs, rotten ruins and giant buried bones that came from the End.

* A set of biomes (-0.45--0.15 temp, -1.0--0.1 humidity, 0.3-1.0 continentals, 0.05 to 0.55 erosion, -0.4 to 0.05
  weirdness or something)
    * Three levels at different climate/depth variation
        * Hollow Vale (outer main biome)
        * Ossuary (inner)
        * Marrow Hollows (cave)
    * Custom music and ambient sounds
    * Foggy and ash/end/particle effect
    * Pale gray/bluish water
* Biomes do not spawn zombies etc, but increased skeletons and endermen
* Enemies
    * Bone Whale
        * A large whale skeleton, passive, floating slowly ~15 above ground
        * It summons 5 frys
        * When tamed, can be ridden, needs a saddle
        * When eating a chorus fruit while riding, it teleports between end and overworld with a 1 / 4 scale factor
        * Drops bones and ender marrow
        * Feeding ender marrow heals them
        * Has a calf version
    * Fry
        * A small skeleton fish, aggressive to living entities closer than 5m, or to whoever hurts the host whale
        * Is tempted by chorus fruit and eats them from the player hand instead of attacking, despawning and setting
          tame counter of the whale +1
        * Spawns sparsely across the biome, without a host they aimlessly idle
        * Are scared by cats
        * Drops bones
    * Hopping Skull
        * Pretends to be just another skeleton skull on the floor, but awakes and hops toward the player when close
        * Explodes into dragon breath like cloud and a small non destructive explosion
        * Drops bone and maybe a skull
    * Bone Sludge
        * A small sludge, sliding towards the player, and when close charges and jumps the player
        * Drops slime, bone, and maybe a skull
    * Skull Sludge
        * A larger sludge, sliding towards the player
        * Bites when close, and throws slime balls at the player causing slowness when further away
        * Dissolves into 3 bone sludges on death
        * Drops slime, bone, a skull
    * Ender Skull
        * A floating skull with an ender eye in its center
        * If it sees the player for 3 consecutive seconds, screams and charges the player
        * Drops a pearl
    * Scavenger
        * The equivalent to a wandering trader but on a skeleton horse, equipped with a cross bow
        * Despawns when half on health using chorus fruit
        * Trades end and otherwise rare or unfarmable items (including marrow and maps to nearby villages or pois)
    * Undead highland cow
        * It's a skeleton cow and has absolutely no reason to be here tbh
        * Cow behavior in herds of 3-5, but they never eat, and from time to time walk to another cow to stay together
        * Bone and leather drops
        * Bone rattle noises and they are oddly interested in the player, tilting their head from time to time and
          staring the player down
        * Unlike animals tho they attack when harmed unless half life
* Items
    * Ender Marrow
    * Potion of levitation (from ender marrow)
    * Bone Whale Omelette (smelted from a whale egg, lots of food)
    * Ender Fish Soup (or something with ender marrow and chorus fruit)
    * Custom music disk
    * Spawn eggs
    * The Whale Harpoon
        * Ancient weapon found or crafted from bone, string, and ender marrow
        * Shoots, similar to the trident, but less damage and reels the enemy towards you
        * Allows off hand + main hand usage for advanced combat
    * Bone Mask
        * A carved bone mask having the same effect as a carved pumpkin, but with a better view and armor
        * Frys wont attack you
    * Bucket of fry
* Blocks
    * Bone bricks, walls, stairs, slabs, pillars, chiseled
    * Inlined ornamented bone bricks (from bone and ender marrow)
    * Marrow Lamp - A faint glowing torch
    * Ender Infused Grass - has a faint ender pulsing animation - Caused by ender marrow of dead whales seeping into it
      on death
    * Whale egg
        * Can be found at whale ribcage sites as brush loot and placed
        * Cracks while it sits and hatches into a whale calf, which is tamed by the nearest player
* Structures
    * Various smaller Ruins
    * A ruined tower
    * A ruined temple
    * An abandoned village
    * Dead trees
    * Ancient dead tree
    * Whale ribcage and 3 different "impact" with ender infused grass and ribcages
* Integration
    * Archelogy and gravel added in most structures
    * Advancements for riding a whale
